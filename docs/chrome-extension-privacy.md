# Privacy policy of the Fettle extension for Chrome

The Fettle extension has one purpose: it shares your sign-in for one site with a bot on a Fettle server that you choose.

## What the extension reads

- The address of the open tab, when you click the Fettle icon.
- The cookies of one site, after you click **Share sign-in** and Chrome gives access to that site.
- The local storage of the open page of that site, at the same time.

The extension reads nothing in the background. It reads no other site and no browsing history.

## Where the data goes

- The extension sends the cookies and the local storage only to the Fettle server that you connected.
- The connection uses the address of that server. Use HTTPS for a server on another computer.
- The Fettle server puts the data into the browser of the bot that you select, and keeps no copy.
- The extension sends no data to the makers of Fettle or to any other party.

## What the extension stores

- The address of your Fettle server, your account name and a token that can only share a sign-in.
- Chrome keeps them on this device. **Disconnect** in the extension removes them.

## Who can use a shared sign-in

Every person who can use the bot in its space can ask the bot to act with your sign-in. The extension shows the number of these people before you share.
To remove a sign-in, open the bot page in Fettle and click **Sign out** next to the site.
