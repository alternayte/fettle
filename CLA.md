# Fettle Contributor Licence Agreement

This agreement applies to each contribution to the Fettle project at <https://github.com/alternayte/fettle>.
It has an individual form and a corporate form.
Read it before you open a pull request.

## Why the project needs this agreement

The core of Fettle is under the GNU Affero General Public License version 3 only.
The `ee` directory is under the Fettle Enterprise License.
The project also gives Fettle under commercial licences.
To do this, the project needs the right to give your contribution under more than one licence.
You keep the copyright of your contribution.

## How to sign

A CLA bot checks each pull request.
When you have not signed, the bot adds a comment to the pull request.
Follow the instructions in that comment to sign.
You sign one time. The signature applies to all your later contributions.
If you contribute for your employer or for another legal entity, that entity signs the corporate form first.

## Definitions

- **"You"** is the person or the legal entity that signs this agreement.
- **"The Project"** is the Fettle project and the maintainers of its repository who act for it.
- **"Contribution"** is any work that you submit to the Project, such as code, documentation, tests, images or bot templates. It includes changes to existing work.
- **"Submit"** means to send a work in any form to the Project. Examples are a pull request, a patch, an issue comment or a message. It does not include a work that you mark in writing as "Not a Contribution".
- **"Corporate Contributor"** is a legal entity that signs the corporate form for its employees and its representatives.

## Corporate form

The Corporate Contributor accepts the terms of the individual form for each Contribution that its designated employees and representatives submit.
In the corporate form, "You" in sections 1 to 7 is the Corporate Contributor.

The Corporate Contributor also agrees to these terms:

1. It gives the Project a list of the persons that can submit Contributions for it. It can give their GitHub organization instead.
2. It tells the Project when the list changes.
3. It states that each designated person has the permission of the Corporate Contributor to submit Contributions.
4. It states that the person who signs the corporate form has the authority to bind the Corporate Contributor.

A designated person also signs the individual form, or the CLA bot accepts that person through the corporate form.
